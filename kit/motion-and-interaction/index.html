Motion and interaction

Four durations, two easings, and motion that always ends on a still frame. Every input works: touch, mouse, trackpad, keyboard and pen.

Tokens

Token Value
--dur-quick 120 ms
--dur-settle 240 ms
--dur-breath 480 ms
--dur-loader 1600 ms
--ease-arrive cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)
--ease-leave cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-MO-01 house review Every animation ends on a still frame that works on its own.
R-MO-02 house lint Durations are 120, 240, 480 and 1600 ms; easings are arrive and leave. Nothing else.
R-MO-03 house lint Reduced motion removes movement only. Opacity and color fades stay, at 120 ms. Never a blanket transition: none.
R-MO-04 house review Motion stays below the words. Text never moves while someone reads it.
R-MO-05 house review Nothing flashes more than twice a second. Only loaders loop.
R-MO-06 house review Signature motion by position: typodojo letters draw themselves in stroke order; Pulp's highlight sweeps once in 240 ms; Thinkwell acknowledges state with thin rings and fades.
R-MO-07 house review Jitter is fixed at its root, never by removing the effect. Motion is smooth on every browser, Safari included.
R-INT-01 house review Every control works with touch, mouse, trackpad, keyboard and pen, with hover and without.
R-INT-02 house review Anything shown on hover also shows on focus and on tap.
R-INT-03 house lint Custom cursors only for fine pointers that can hover. Text keeps the I-beam.
R-INT-04 house review Hover or tap previews; click, Enter or a second tap selects; arrows move; Escape clears.
R-INT-05 house review Jumps take about 400 ms and land below where the bar sits, whether it is showing or folded. Reduced motion makes them instant.
R-INT-06 pulp review The bar has two states. At the top of a page it is the whole bar. Scrolling down folds it into a round glass button at the bottom right, 96 px with the whole wordmark inside at 72 px; a scroll up of 12 px or more brings the whole bar back, and an 8 px dead zone stops it flickering. The button steps aside while a mark crosses its corner, 16 px clear of it: the footer's wordmark (so the wordmark is always on screen, at the top or the bottom, and never twice), the robot, and anything marked data-keep-clear. Marks never sit under it; text and photos may scroll beneath its glass. Hover, focus or a click opens its menu above it: the nav, the mode switch and Get in touch; on touch a tap opens the menu sheet. Reduced motion swaps the two without movement.
R-ANIM-01 house lint Every animation and transition is listed in motion.json with its trigger, what moves, duration, easing, loop and reduced-motion behavior. @keyframes names match motion.json ids.
R-ANIM-02 house lint Durations and easings only through var(--dur-) and var(--ease-).
R-ANIM-03 house lint Only opacity, transform, colors, box-shadow, background-size, underline thickness and outline color may animate. Never width, height, top, left or margin.
R-ANIM-04 house lint Under prefers-reduced-motion, transforms stop and fades stay at 120 ms. Never transition: none.
R-ANIM-05 house review Scroll-triggered motion runs once per element per visit, through IntersectionObserver, never a scroll listener. Content is visible without JavaScript.
R-ANIM-06 house review The robot blinks at most three times a visit, at least 8 s apart, only while the hero is in view and the page is idle.
R-INTX-01 house review Every interactive component is listed in interactions.json with its element, states, keyboard path, touch path, ARIA and motion ids.
R-INTX-02 house review Native elements first: a button for actions, a link for navigation, a labeled input for answers. A role only where nothing native fits.
R-INTX-03 house review Dialogs and sheets move focus in, trap it, close on Escape, outside click and the close button, and return focus to the control that opened them.
R-INTX-04 house review The mode switch is a group of three radios. It follows the system first, then remembers the choice, and changes colors only. It lives in the bar and its menus, never in the footer.
R-INTX-05 house review Forms check a field when it is left and when the form is sent, never on every key. Errors say what to do, in words with Akira red beside them; done says what happened, with Alan Green beside it.
R-INTX-06 house review Keyboard order follows reading order, and a skip link comes first.

Contract

From pack/contracts.ts.

type Duration = 120 | 240 | 480 | 1600;  // quick, settle, breath, loader
type Easing = 'arrive' | 'leave';
// arrive: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)
// leave:  cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)

type Motion = {
  duration: Duration;
  easing: Easing;
  endsOnStill: true;
  loops: boolean;                // loaders only
  reduced: 'end-frame' | 'fade-120';
};

type Input = 'touch' | 'mouse' | 'trackpad' | 'keyboard' | 'pen';
type Pointer = { fine: boolean; canHover: boolean };

type Reveal = {
  preview: ['hover', 'focus', 'tap'];   // all three, always
  select: ['click', 'Enter', 'second-tap'];
  clear: 'Escape';
};
