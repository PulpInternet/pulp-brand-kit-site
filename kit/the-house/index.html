01 · The kit

The house

One house, three positions. The dial says what may change between typodojo, Pulp and Thinkwell; the invariants say what never does.

The dial

Every Pulp property sits on one line. typodojo is less formal, Pulp is the house, and Thinkwell is more formal. Publishing (essays, Pulp101, data stories such as From Stone to Screen) sits at zero with Pulp.

Property Position Where Endorsement Signature People come to
typodojo -1 Less formal A Pulp special project Typo, the dojo's dog Practice and play
Pulp 0 The house None. Pulp signs itself The gum robot Read and explore
Thinkwell 1 More formal A Pulp product The tw cloud Work and decide
Publishing 0 With the house Published by Pulp One accent per part Read

Twelve parameters move with the dial

Anything a property does not set comes from Pulp.

Ten invariants never move

Invariant Rule
American spelling. No em or en dashes. R-TXT-01
No italics in any display type. R-TYPE-02
Light, dark and mono, and the switch test. R-MODE-02
44 px targets and every kind of input. R-LAY-04
Secondary views float as modals. R-CMP-05
Focus by brightness. R-CMP-07
Gradients only from tokens. No edge stripes. R-COL-08
Every number has a unit and a source. R-DATA-01
Pulp is always the full wordmark. R-LOGO-01
Real people are never dithered. R-IMG-02

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-HOUSE-01 house review Pulp is the house. Every property is endorsed by Pulp, inherits every house rule, and may move only the parameters on the dial.
R-HOUSE-02 house review The dial has three positions: typodojo at minus one, less formal; Pulp at zero; Thinkwell at plus one, more formal. A new property is placed on the dial before any design work starts.
R-HOUSE-03 house lint A parameter a property does not set takes Pulp's value. tokens.json resolves every property against Pulp first.
R-HOUSE-04 house review No property borrows another's signature: Typo, the gum robot, the tw cloud, Pink Matter and typodojo's prints stay home.
R-HOUSE-05 house review Publishing (essays, Pulp101, data stories) sits at zero, signs as Published by Pulp, and may give each part its own accent.
R-HOUSE-06 house review After Hours sits off the dial: never on the corporate domain, in press, or in investor material.
R-HOUSE-07 house review The ten invariants never move, whatever a property's position.

Contract

From pack/contracts.ts.

type PropertyId = 'typodojo' | 'pulp' | 'thinkwell' | 'publishing';
type Dial = -1 | 0 | 1;          // less formal, the house, more formal

type Property = {
  id: PropertyId;
  dial: Dial;                    // publishing sits at 0
  endorsement: 'A Pulp special project' | 'A Pulp product'
             | 'Published by Pulp' | null;   // Pulp signs itself
  signature: 'typo' | 'gum-robot' | 'tw-cloud' | null;
  set: Partial<DialParameters>;  // all else resolves to Pulp (R-HOUSE-03)
};

type DialParameters = {
  displayFace: FaceName;  readingFace: FaceName;
  case: 'lower' | 'caps-hero' | 'sentence';
  headlineEnds: 'period' | 'period-on-statements' | 'none';
  radii: number[];        accentBudget: 0 | 1;
  character: 'typo' | 'gum-robot' | null;
  texture: 'prints' | 'plates' | 'none';
  motion: 'self-draw' | 'highlight-sweep' | 'state-rings';
  density: 'card' | 'column' | 'panels';
  voice: 'warm' | 'essay' | 'exact';
};

// The ten invariants are not parameters. Nothing sets them.
type Invariant = typeof INVARIANTS[number];
