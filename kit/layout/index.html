07 · The kit

Layout

One reading column of 680, a frame of 1180, and a fixed spacing scale. No sideways scroll and no scroll inside scroll.

Scales

Scale Values
Space (px) 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 56, 64, 96
Radii, typodojo 8, 12, 24, 999 (999 is a pill)
Radii, Pulp 0, 6, 999
Radii, Thinkwell 2, 4, 6, 10, 16, 24
Widths column 680, frame pulp 1180, frame typodojo 1240, gutter 24, gutter phone 16
Z index rail 45, bar 50, modal 80

CSS breakpoints (min-width) 560, 768, 1024, 1280

Targets 44 px on touch, 24 px floor anywhere, 8 px between two targets

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-LAY-01 house review Two widths: the 680 reading column and the frame (Pulp 1180, typodojo 1240). Thinkwell follows its own frame rules (R-CMP-14).
R-LAY-02 house lint Spacing comes from one scale: 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 56, 64, 96. Use gap between siblings, not margins.
R-LAY-13 house layout One left edge. Every heading, label and grid in a section starts on the column's left edge at every width; rows of a grid share a top edge; a grid never ends on one lonely item under a fuller row. On tablets and large phones, plates sit as rows: the plate left, its words right.
R-LAY-03 house lint Radii by position: typodojo 8, 12, 24 for cards, and pill; Pulp 0 for bands and plates, 6 for cards and swatches, pill for buttons and chips; Thinkwell 2, 4, 6, 10, 16, 24.
R-LAY-04 house layout Every target is at least 44 by 44 under 1024 px wide, under 500 px tall, or on coarse pointers, and never under 24 anywhere.
R-LAY-05 house layout No page-level sideways scroll, and no scrolling region inside a scrolling page.
R-LAY-06 house layout Fixed bars respect safe areas, and their middle lines up with the reading column within 2 px.
R-LAY-07 house layout Photos and figures start on the text's left edge. A figure narrower than the column is never centered.
R-LAY-08 house layout Checks run at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 2000 px wide, portrait and landscape, at 200 percent zoom, with forced colors, in every mode.
R-LAY-09 house lint z-index takes three values: rail 45, bar 50, modal 80.
