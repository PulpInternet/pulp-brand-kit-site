04 · The kit

Color

Every color has a name, one job and a place it never goes. The names are on brand: printing and paper for the neutrals, the people who chose them for the accents.

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-COL-01 house lint Palette colors only. Every value comes from tokens.json. Accents keep the name of the teammate who chose them.
R-COL-02 house lint One accent per object, and one resting highlight per page: the Highlighter block. Pulp and publishing use the full palette this way; Thinkwell keeps one accent per palette; typodojo uses none.
R-COL-10 pulp review Each named accent marks its own kind of object wherever it appears: Damani Cyan the machine and simulated personas, Uday Wintergreen real people and In Person, Akira red urgency, errors and see, Alan Green success and things kept, Rifaz Blue guidance and hints, Pat Teal value and cost, Milana Blue research and evidence, Wen Blue links, citations and the first series, Nicole Steel large figures and lines, Halftone and Bubble the plates and quiet bands, Stick physical goods.
R-COL-11 pulp review Dark mode gets as much care as light. On Noir, accents switch to their dark partners: Bubble links and plates, Wintergreen bright for lines and type.
R-COL-03 pulp review Covers on Newsprint, Pulp Stock, Lithograph or Noir. Never on Bubble, Blacklight or Halftone.
R-COL-04 pulp lint Highlighter is a flat block behind one headline word or the current item, with Noir text. Never text on Newsprint, never on an image.
R-COL-05 house layout Text on an accent fill is Noir. Text passes 4.5:1 and marks pass 3:1; add an edge token rather than darkening a brand color.
R-COL-06 thinkwell lint Pink Matter, settled: #C361BC for the mark; #9A3D94 as the UI accent on light and #E29ADC on dark. #DA6FD5 is retired.
R-COL-07 house review Context colors stay on their own pages: Hannah greens on sustainability, Pink Matter and Gray Matter only where Thinkwell is named.
R-COL-08 house lint No glows and no colored stripe on one edge of a card, modal, callout or panel. Gradients are written only through the --grad- and --glass- tokens; nothing else may fade, glow or shade.
R-COL-13 pulp review One page, one scheme. Structure is neutral at Newsprint's hue: Newsprint, Pulp Stock, Lithograph, Noir. Every picture is in one violet ink, Halftone (Bubble on dark). Highlighter is the one complement, used once. Accents carry meaning in small marks only: a dot, a line, a link.
R-COL-14 pulp review Proportion 60, 30, 10: the ground, then type and bands, then imagery and the one highlight. A page has at most two Pulp Stock bands and one feature block, and no two bands touch.
R-COL-15 pulp review One feature block per page, for the call to action: Halftone on light and dark, Noir on mono, with Newsprint type, an inverted button and the robot in Bubble.
R-COL-16 house layout Every piece of text takes its color from a mode role, never a fixed hex and never from inheritance alone. Switched with the page's own control, under a host stylesheet, every text holds 4.5:1 against what is behind it in light, dark and mono (3:1 at 24 px, or 18.66 px bold, and up). The one fixed pair is Noir on Highlighter.
R-COL-09 typodojo lint typodojo is black and white: its black, its white, and its gray ramp. No hue anywhere, ever.

Contract

From pack/contracts.ts.

type Hex = `#${string}`;
type Ground = 'newsprint' | 'noir' | 'carbon-copy' | 'pulp-stock' | 'bubble';

type ColorToken = {
  token: string;                 // --pulp-<token>
  hex: Hex;
  tier: 'neutral' | 'tint' | 'accent' | 'context' | 'property';
  owner?: 'Damani' | 'Uday' | 'Wen' | 'Nicole' | 'Hannah' | 'Alan' | 'Rifaz' | 'Milana' | 'Pat';
  role: string;                  // one job
  never: string;
  scope: PropertyId[];
  contrast: Record<Ground, number>;  // measured, WCAG
  textOn: Ground[];              // only where contrast >= 4.5
};

type AccentBudget = {
  pulp: 1;        // resting highlights per page; one accent per object
  publishing: 1;  // per part, plus one accent per object
  thinkwell: 1;   // per palette
  typodojo: 0;    // none, ever
};

const RETIRED: Hex[] = ['#DA6FD5'];
