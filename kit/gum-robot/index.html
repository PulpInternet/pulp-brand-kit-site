The gum robot

Pulp's one character. Drawn once, recolored by mode, never redrawn.

How it behaves

The gum robot is there, helpful and out of the way. It understates how smart it is. It is calm, focused and unbothered, and it waits until something is needed. It never watches, listens, speaks or boasts.

Where it goes

  • Hero, cropped off the edge, at most 40 vw.
  • The CTA band, where it peeks up once and stays.
  • About, 404 and merch.
  • One per screen, at least 96 px wide. Pulp only: never in Thinkwell or typodojo, never next to a face, never as a persona.

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-BOT-01 pulp review The gum robot is Pulp's only character and stays at zero. It never appears in Thinkwell, and Typo never appears on Pulp.
R-BOT-02 pulp review The robot is calm help: headphones on, blowing a gum bubble inside a speech bubble, a play on chat bubble and bubble gum. Unbothered, focused, never boasting. It is drawn once, recolored by tokens, never redrawn, restyled or named.
R-BOT-03 pulp review Places: the hero, one CTA peek, Company > About, the 404, merch, Plate 11, the home share card, and the favicon (its head and gum alone, on a Noir tile). One per screen at most. Its world (the headphones, the gum, the sparkles, the cloud) may appear without it, on Plates 12 and 13 and the Robot weather print. Never beside a face, never as a persona or an avatar.
R-BOT-04 pulp layout At least 96 px wide on screen. In a hero, at most 40 percent of the viewport width.
R-BOT-05 pulp lint Line work is the imagery ink, the same as the plates: Halftone on light, Bubble on dark, Smudge on mono, and Bubble or Smudge in the feature block. The face fill matches the ground. Never an accent color, never the outlined sticker.
R-BOT-06 pulp review It blinks at most once every 8 s and never loops. A peek rises in 480 ms with the arrive easing and stays. Reduced motion: already in place, no blink.
R-BOT-07 pulp review It never speaks or shows off: no lines, quotes, or captions in its voice, and never framed as watching, listening to, or knowing about the person. Speaking characters belong to typodojo.

Contract

From pack/contracts.ts.

type BotPlacement = 'hero' | 'cta-peek' | 'about' | '404' | 'merch';

type GumRobotUse = {
  property: 'pulp';              // never 'thinkwell' or 'typodojo'
  placement: BotPlacement;
  perScreen: 1;                  // at most
  widthPx: number;               // >= 96; hero <= 40vw
  line: 'noir' | 'newsprint';   // by mode
  face: 'ground';                // the face fill matches the ground
  motion: 'still' | 'blink' | 'peek';
  blinkEveryMs?: number;         // >= 8000, never loops
  peekMs?: 480;                  // arrive easing, then stays
  speaks: false;                 // no lines, ever
  framedAs: 'calm-help';         // never watching or listening
  besideFace: false;
  asPersona: false;
};

// typodojo's Typo has its own contract in the typodojo repo.
