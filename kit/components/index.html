08 · The kit

Components

One button system, one switch, one step marker, one way to float a view. The control heights are 24, 32, 40 and 44.

Step markers

State Opacity
done 0.55
now 1
next 0.75

Every screen draws six states

Loading, empty, offline, error, long and short.

Floating views

Secondary views float: a modal on desktop, a sheet on phones. They dim the page, close on outside click, the close button and Escape, and return focus to the opener.

Rules

ID Scope Checked by Rule
R-CMP-01 house lint Controls are 24, 32, 40 or 44 tall. Nothing else.
R-CMP-02 house review One primary button per view: Noir fill on light, Newsprint fill on dark and mono. Secondary is an outline in the line color.
R-CMP-03 house lint Buttons and chips are pills on Pulp and typodojo. In Thinkwell, controls have 10 px corners, and the Ask button inside the 24 px Ask bar has 16.
R-CMP-04 house lint One switch: 40 by 24 with a 16 px knob set 4 px in. On is the type color; off is a ringed well.
R-CMP-05 house review Secondary views float: a modal on desktop, a sheet from the bottom on phones, over a dimmed page. They close on outside click, the close button and Escape, and return focus to the control that opened them. Never a page jump.
R-CMP-06 house review Two surfaces never overlap at full strength. The one underneath dims or blurs.
R-CMP-07 house review Focus by brightness: done at 55 percent, now at full strength, next at 75. Everything lights up together at a finish.
R-CMP-08 house review One step marker: a 24 px circle. Done shows a check, now is filled with the type color, next keeps its number.
R-CMP-09 house review Rows divide with a half-pixel line. Shadows only for floating views and lifted cards.
R-CMP-10 house review States keep the frame. Loading says what already works; an error says what happened, that nothing is lost, and offers Try again.
R-CMP-11 house layout Long text truncates to one line with the full text on hover and focus. Numbers never truncate; counts over 99 read 99+.
R-CMP-12 house review Search is one command menu that searches everything, and its field never moves while results appear.
R-CMP-13 house review Menus open one short move from the pointer, never directly under it.
R-CMP-14 thinkwell layout The Thinkwell frame follows its own size: full panel from 1280, a rail from 1024, one column under 1024, phone header and bottom tabs under 768.

Contract

From pack/contracts.ts.

type ControlHeight = 24 | 32 | 40 | 44;

type Button = {
  kind: 'primary' | 'secondary' | 'link';
  height: ControlHeight;
  shape: 'pill' | 'r10';         // pill: Pulp, typodojo. r10: Thinkwell
  primaryPerView: 1;
};

type FloatingView = {
  desktop: 'modal'; phone: 'sheet';
  dimsPage: true;
  closesOn: ['outside', 'close', 'Escape'];
  returnsFocus: true;
};

type StepState = 'done' | 'now' | 'next';
const STEP_OPACITY = { done: 0.55, now: 1, next: 0.75 };

type ScreenState = 'loading' | 'empty' | 'offline' | 'error'
                 | 'long' | 'short';      // every screen draws all six
