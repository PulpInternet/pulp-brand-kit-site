15 · The kit

The website

pulp.com.ai, reimagined: 45 pages in 8 sections, plus the views every page shares. Each page has its purpose, audience, contents, action, visual and template.

How every page is built

Every page is one of twelve templates, and a few pages list their own sections where the template lacks one. A template is an ordered list of section kinds, and every section kind is built from parts that already run on the landing page or are specified below with their interaction record in pack/interactions.json. The machine-readable version, page by page, is pack/site.json; the types are the site block of pack/contracts.ts.

The shell

  • Bar: the Pulp wordmark, 6 links (Research, Laboratory, Platform, Foundation, What we do, Company), then the mode switch, search and Get in touch. On phones: Get in touch and the menu sheet.
  • Footer: 7 groups (Research, Laboratory, Platform, Foundation, What we do, Company, Trust), then the wordmark, EST. 2026, Brooklyn, New York, and the credit for every photo on the page.
  • On every page: the skip link, the contact modal, search, and the menu sheet.
  • Files: tokens.css, site.css, site.js, and the folders img/, marks/ and plates/, cached across pages.
  • Budget: 200 KB gzip per page, photos excluded. Per page, photos excluded. The single-file landing page is 198 KB gzip with every plate inlined; the multi-page site serves plates, marks and fonts as cached files instead of data URIs.
  • Without JavaScript: Every page reads and navigates without JavaScript: the mode follows the system setting, the contact modal falls back to /company/contact, search falls back to the footer map, records render inline as details elements, and every form posts as a plain HTML form.
  • Storage: The saved mode is the only thing the site stores, in localStorage, wrapped in try/catch. No cookies, no third-party scripts, no analytics beacons.
  • Search: a static index, search.json, written by site_pages.py with title, path, group, summary for every entry, grouped as Pages, Papers, Stories, Docs.
  • Conversations: Every action that starts a conversation (Get in touch, Invite us, Apply, Partner with the Foundation, Contact press, Talk to us, Report a barrier, Report a vulnerability) opens the contact modal with its topic preset; there is one inbox.

Pages under a list are long reads: /research/stories/<slug>, /lab/pulp101/letters/<slug>, /lab/pulp101/numbers/<slug>. Papers, talks, roles, campaigns and people are records: each renders inline as a details element and, with script, opens in a floating modal with a shareable hash. A record never has a page of its own.
