  1. R-HOUSE-01

    Pulp is the house. Every property is endorsed by Pulp, inherits every house rule, and may move only the parameters on the dial.

    housereview
  2. R-HOUSE-02

    The dial has three positions: typodojo at minus one, less formal; Pulp at zero; Thinkwell at plus one, more formal. A new property is placed on the dial before any design work starts.

    housereview
  3. R-HOUSE-03

    A parameter a property does not set takes Pulp's value. tokens.json resolves every property against Pulp first.

    houselint
  4. R-HOUSE-04

    No property borrows another's signature: Typo, the gum robot, the tw cloud, Pink Matter and typodojo's prints stay home.

    housereview
  5. R-HOUSE-05

    housereview

  16. R-LOGO-09

    The platform is two SDKs, each with its own mark. The Pulp API is the attention orb (Q-cubies3-30): the shell of a 3 by 3 by 3 cube of particles rounded toward a sphere, the attention X corner to corner on its front faces in the type color, the near shell in --c-api (Nicole Steel) and the back in --c-api-far; then the full Pulp wordmark, then API in Libre Franklin 800. At 32 px and under it is the flat cut, a 3 by 3 grid with both diagonals heavy. Its motion, a turn that breathes between cube and orb, runs on one canvas on the platform section and /platform/api only, and stands still under reduced motion. Loosethreads is cloth: warp in the type color, weft in Blacklight, and its last thread dyed Pat Teal and pulled loose into a bight (LT-a), then its name and the line Part of the Pulp platform. In mono the orb and the threads go to grays. LT-e, the long thread under the name, is the alternate lockup; LT-c, one thread, is the favicon.

  39. R-COL-16

    Every piece of text takes its color from a mode role, never a fixed hex and never from inheritance alone. Switched with the page's own control, under a host stylesheet, every text holds 4.5:1 against what is behind it in light, dark and mono (3:1 at 24 px, or 18.66 px bold, and up).     houselayout
  40. R-GRD-01

    Three gradients, all tokens: --grad-hero (a glow of the mode's --c-glow, 8 to 28 percent, over a ground that settles into the band), --grad-scrim (the photo ground rising 60 to 90 percent under a caption) and --grad-feature (the feature color solid for its first 30 to 45 percent, then thinning over a photo).

    pulplint
  41. R-GRD-02

    A gradient is light, not a color of its own. It runs between two roles of the same mode, never across two hues, and text sits only on its solid end.

  42. R-PHO-01

    Photos are free to use under a license recorded in assets/source/photos.json with the photographer and the source page. Each band credits its photographer by name only in its caption (R-IMG-12), the feature block in its corner, and the footer lists them all, with no license line on the page. Originals stay out of git.

    pulpreview
  43. R-PHO-02

    What Pulp photographs: night, light and people in conversation or in crowds, never posed stock, never a screen of a product, never a face we cannot credit. Night blues, violets and sodium golds, so they sit with Halftone and Highlighter.

    pulpreview
  44. R-PHO-03

At most three bands and breaks a page, never two touching, never in a card or beside text. A band or break caption sits on the scrim, in Newsprint, one line at heading size. The hero photo has no caption and is credited in the footer.
